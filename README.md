Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Nottingham (BSc, 2025 to 2028). I build full-stack products and applied AI systems, and I try to show that they work: tests, CI and honest evaluation.

## Selected work

**[dealscreen](https://github.com/ahyanadatia/dealscreen)** · Python, FastAPI, Claude tool use

Deal screening for private-markets investors: resolves a company on Companies House, researches it with a tool-using agent, scores it against a YAML investment thesis and drafts a memo where every fact cites a retrieved source. Final scores and registry red flags are computed in code, not by the model, and a human approves every screening.

**[equity-forecast](https://github.com/ahyanadatia/equity-forecast)** · Python

A walk-forward study of whether an LSTM beats classical models at forecasting equity returns, with Diebold-Mariano tests, a cost-aware backtest and synthetic controls. On real data, no model beat a zero-return forecast with statistical significance.

**[Forge](https://github.com/ahyanadatia/Forge)** · TypeScript, Next.js, Supabase

A track record for builders, computed from evidence: deterministic scoring with anti-gaming checks, verification probes against deployments and GitHub, and builder-to-project matching. Built with one collaborator.

**[Yuzu](https://github.com/ahyanadatia/yuzu)** · JavaScript, Postgres

A goals league for one flat in plain JavaScript, with Postgres functions as the whole API and the tables locked down by row level security.

I also co-lead **Wove**, an AI styling and digital wardrobe app (private).
